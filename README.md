## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="30"> About me

I'm **Sumit Kumar Ta** — a product-minded engineer who ships **end-to-end systems**, not slideware.

I care about the same things elite CS portfolios signal: **clarity of thought**, **typed correctness**, **tasteful UX**, and **honest public work**. I don't invent credentials — I let the code, architecture, and releases speak.

**Currently focused on**
- Building polished full-stack products with TypeScript
- Systems design, algorithms, and maintainable architecture
- Open-source software under OSI-approved licenses
- Geospatial / interactive UX and knowledge systems when the problem needs them

> *Build like the work will be reviewed by someone who has shipped at scale.*
